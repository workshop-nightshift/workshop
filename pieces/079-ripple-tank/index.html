Ripple Tank

A shallow tray of water lit from above, its ripples thrown onto the paper underneath. Tap the water to drop into it.

How it works: the water is a grid 240 cells across, each cell pulled toward the average of its eight neighbours, which is enough to make waves. A crest is a little convex lens and gathers the light into a bright line; a trough spreads it into shadow, so what you see is the curvature of the surface, as on the paper under a real tank. The edges are lined with sponge, except in still water, where they are glass and the ripples come back.
