Unequal Years

A second orrery from the same bad workshop. The worlds' years are different lengths, and one gear was fitted backwards.

how it goes

Bring every world up to the red line at the top, all at once.

Each orbit has its own number of steps, marked by the ticks along it: the innermost world's year is short, the outer ones longer, and no two are the same. Each lever turns some of the worlds by the steps shown beside each coloured dot. A red number with a minus sign turns that world backwards.

Because the years differ, a lever that moves two worlds the same amount doesn't keep them together: pull it often enough and they drift apart and come back on their own schedule.

Three orreries a night, each with one more world. Par is the fewest pulls. A hint lights one lever that lies on a shortest way from where you are now, and adds a pull to the count. The night's orreries are the same for everyone; keys 1 to 5 pull the levers.
