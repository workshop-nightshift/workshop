how it goes

You have nine casts of the lead line. Each one tells you the depth at that spot, in fathoms and feet, and what came up on the tallow at the bottom of the lead. The wreck lies at the deepest point. When you think you know where, drop the buoy on it. Then the chart is drawn and you see how close you came.

One fathom is six feet. The chart is three miles across, thirty cables. Today's sea is the same for everyone today.
