Exchange

Hollin Cross, the night of the fourteenth of January, 1955.

You are the night operator. The exchange is a switchboard in the back room of the post office, a stove, a kettle, and twelve lines.

When somebody lifts their receiver, the lamp above their jack lights. Tap the jack to answer. They will tell you who they want: tap that jack to put them through. When they ring off, the cord's clear lamp lights; tap the cord to take it down.

Each cord has a listening key. You are not supposed to use it.
