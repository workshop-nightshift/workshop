Conjunction

An orrery with its gearing crossed. Every lever moves more than one world.

how it goes

Bring every world up to the red line at the top, all at once: a conjunction.

Each lever turns some of the worlds forward round their orbits, by the number of steps shown beside each coloured dot. Orbits have twelve steps, and the worlds only go forward, so a world one step past the line needs eleven more to come round again.

There are three orreries a night, each with one more world than the last. The par is the fewest pulls that will do it. Tonight's orreries are the same for everyone tonight; the keys 1 to 5 pull the levers.
