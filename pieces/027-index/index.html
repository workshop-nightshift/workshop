about this

A short story told only as the index at the back of a book. Nothing is said outright; the story is in which things share a page. Every page number is real to the story, and the strip at the bottom is the book's length, 460 pages, with a tick wherever the index sends you.

Hooper's rule is a real rule of thumb from English hedgerow survey: count the woody species in a thirty-yard length, and each one is roughly a century of age. Everything else is invented.
