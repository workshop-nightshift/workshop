Both Ways

Three short poems that read down, and then back up.

same lines, the other way
One phrase per line. It turns as you type.

A reverso is a poem whose lines, taken in the opposite order, make a second poem. Nothing is added or taken away going back up; only the stops and capitals move, and they are marked as they change.

The trick is in lines that can lean either way: a list, a place, a phrase that waits to see what it belongs to.
