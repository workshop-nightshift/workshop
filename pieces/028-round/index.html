Round

Letterpress again, but the line is sung as a round. The first voice starts; the next comes in a few beats behind, singing the same letters, and the next behind that. Every key sits on the same five-note scale, so wherever the voices fall against each other, they agree.

Letters as in Letterpress: vowels ring, consonants pluck, commas breathe, full stops land home. First voice in the middle, the second an octave down, the third an octave up, the fourth low and slow. Voices leave in the order they came, the way a round ends.
