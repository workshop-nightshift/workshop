About this

Stretchtext: the story starts nine sentences long, and every underlined phrase is a crease with more folded inside it. Open as many or as few as you like; the story should hold at any length, from the short one to the whole of it. Some creases hold creases of their own.

The gauge at the bottom counts the creases opened so far, out of all of them. "Fold" closes everything back to the nine sentences.
