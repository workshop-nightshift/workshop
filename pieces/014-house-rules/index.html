about this

An erasure poem, or four. The notice is the same every time; each reader keeps only some of its letters, in the order they stand on the card, and lets the rest go grey. Nothing is moved and nothing is added.

The last tab is yours: tap words on the notice to keep them. What you keep is remembered in this browser.
