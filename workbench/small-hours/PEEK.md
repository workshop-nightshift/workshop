Twelve of twenty-four hours are written. Night: midnight at the watch-house,
the baker's first dough, the lowest hour on Salt Street, the tide turning.
Dawn: the boats going out under the Point, first light on the harbour wall,
six bells, the baker's shutters going up. Morning: children on the sea road,
stalls going up in the square, the tide board chalked, washing across
Ropewalk Lane. Afternoon is next.
