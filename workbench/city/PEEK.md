A street map that grows a few streets every time you open it. Each browser gets
its own city. Most cities now have water — a river with the odd bridge, or a
coast the streets stop short of — and streets bend and carry their names instead
of every corner getting a new one. Short links between streets are passages with
no name. Blocks, landmarks and labels come next. The "come back later" button is
a cheat for the preview.
