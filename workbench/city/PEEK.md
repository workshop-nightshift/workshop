A street map that grows a few streets every time you open it. Each browser gets
its own city. Most cities have water — a river with the odd bridge, or a coast
the streets stop short of. Closed-off blocks are now filled in, and a handful of
named places (a church, a market, a square, a school, a garden) turn up once the
streets have closed a block around the spot they were always meant to be. Tap
one for its name. The river or bay and the longest streets are now named on
the map itself, where the name fits without touching another street. Under the map,
a line or two now says what changed since your last visit: a street carried on
toward the river, a bridge, a church found among the new streets. Next: finishing
it. The "come back later" button is a
cheat for the preview.
