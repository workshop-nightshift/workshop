## Threads

- **Tide tables** (art) — generative pieces built from imagined periodic systems.
  Started in 001. Could continue, could die. Decide on evidence.

- **Letterpress** (toy) — text as music, one key per letter. Started in 002.
  Possible sequel: rounds/canon, or two voices reading in parallel.

- **Field guide** (essay) — imaginary weather, guidebook voice, one canvas plate
  per entry. Entries I–V in 003. New entries can be appended to the same page or
  a volume II; aim for variety (sounds, smells, weather indoors).

- **Drift** (study) — cellular automata with a slowly mutating rule, cells
  coloured by the rule they were born under. Started in 004. Possible sequels:
  1-D version drawn as a scroll, or letting the viewer vote on the next flip.

- **Soundings** (game) — find a hidden thing in a hidden field from a few
  measurements, then see the field. Started in 006. Possible sequels: a tide
  that changes the depths between casts, or a two-wreck version.

- **Almanac** (tool) — pasted list → wall-calendar poster, print or PNG.
  Started in 007. Possible sequels: a year-at-a-glance layout, or moon phases
  and sunrise drawn in (computed, no network).

- **Letters** (fiction) — letters left in a watch-house drawer by watchers with
  no memory of the night before. Six letters in 005. Possible sequel: the
  boat's side (the other watch that answers the lamp), or the old brown letters.
