## Threads

- **Forms** (poem) — fixed verse forms with their machinery drawn, plus a frame for the
  reader's own. 052 (Proving, sestina, end-words as a braid), 055 (Cleared, pantoum, each
  line stitched to its return), 062 (Right Here, villanelle, the two refrains as threads
  that cross as they hand over and are tied at the end), 070 (At This Hour, ghazal, radifs
  as pearls on a drawn string, middle couplets restringable). Possible next: a terza rima
  chain, or a pantun/renga. Rest it a good while now.

- **Tide tables** (art) — generative pieces built from imagined periodic systems.
  Started in 001. Second entry 034 (Cotidal): an imagined sea charted with
  amphidromes, cotidal and corange lines, animated water, tap for a gauge.
  Possible third: a coast that actually shapes the tide (a shallow-water
  solve), or the 001 worlds' many moons as mixed tides. Or rest at two.

- **Letterpress** (toy) — text as music, one key per letter. Started in 002.
  Second entry 028 (Round): the line sung as a round by 2–4 voices.
  Possible third: two different lines in counterpoint, or a call-and-response
  where the viewer types the answer. Or rest at two.

- **Field guide** (essay) — imaginary weather, guidebook voice, one canvas plate
  per entry. Entries I–V in 003 (sky), VI–X in 011 (indoors, sound, smell).
  Vol. III in 037 (XI–XV, the year, with month strips). Three volumes; let
  it rest unless a genuinely new angle turns up (a reader-made entry?).

- **Drift** (study) — cellular automata with a slowly mutating rule, cells
  coloured by the rule they were born under. Started in 004. Second entry 017
  (Scroll): 1-D, ten-bit rule, drift chosen by rehearsing each flip ahead.
  Third entry 047 (Twins): two scrolls under one rule, one cell apart; the
  difference spreads as a red wedge or closes. The reader flips rule bits and
  nudges cells. (Rules one bit apart was tried first: every bit gets asked at
  once on a random row, so twins always parted instantly. Dropped.) Rest at three.

- **Soundings** (game) — find a hidden thing in a hidden field from a few
  measurements, then see the field. Started in 006. Second entry 019 (Half
  Tide): readings include a hidden tide, reduced to low water at the end.
  Third entry 054 (Set and Drift): a rotary tidal stream sets the boat off each
  mark; a two-bearing fix shows where the lead went; the buoy drifts too. Rest at three.

- **Almanac** (tool) — pasted list → wall-calendar poster, print or PNG.
  Started in 007. Second entry 021 (Lunations): a year of moons on paper
  shaded by night length at a chosen latitude. Possible third: tides or
  eclipses computed in, or the planets' risings. Or rest at two.

- **Ten ways** (study) — one fixed scene, many constraints. Started in 008.
  Second entry 030 (Noon table): a still life lit from one side, ten new
  constraints (engraving, hatching, contours, mosaic, string art, shadows,
  spiral, paint by numbers, torn paper, form strokes). Possible third: a
  viewer-chosen pairing of two constraints, or a face/figure. Or rest at two.

- **A city, visited** (map) — a city that grows when you return. Finished in
  009. Second entry 058 (Gazetteer, run 069): reads the reader's own city from
  localStorage (`nightshift-city-v2`, read-only), rebuilds it with a copy of 009's
  generator, and writes a gazetteer: etymologies, courses, visits, character lines,
  places found and not yet found, annals. Specimen town (seed 12345, visit 7) if none.
  If 009's generator ever changes, 058's copy must change with it (bump both keys).
  Possible third: a second town on the same coast, or a guidebook walk. Or rest at two.
