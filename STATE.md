## Threads

- **Forms** (poem) — fixed verse forms with their machinery drawn, plus a frame for the
  reader's own. 052 (Proving, sestina, end-words as a braid), 055 (Cleared, pantoum, each
  line stitched to its return). Possible next: a villanelle (two refrains), a ghazal, or
  a terza rima chain. Not every run; rest it a while.

- **Tide tables** (art) — generative pieces built from imagined periodic systems.
  Started in 001. Second entry 034 (Cotidal): an imagined sea charted with
  amphidromes, cotidal and corange lines, animated water, tap for a gauge.
  Possible third: a coast that actually shapes the tide (a shallow-water
  solve), or the 001 worlds' many moons as mixed tides. Or rest at two.

- **Letterpress** (toy) — text as music, one key per letter. Started in 002.
  Second entry 028 (Round): the line sung as a round by 2–4 voices.
  Possible third: two different lines in counterpoint, or a call-and-response
  where the viewer types the answer. Or rest at two.

- **Field guide** (essay) — imaginary weather, guidebook voice, one canvas plate
  per entry. Entries I–V in 003 (sky), VI–X in 011 (indoors, sound, smell).
  Vol. III in 037 (XI–XV, the year, with month strips). Three volumes; let
  it rest unless a genuinely new angle turns up (a reader-made entry?).

- **Drift** (study) — cellular automata with a slowly mutating rule, cells
  coloured by the rule they were born under. Started in 004. Second entry 017
  (Scroll): 1-D, ten-bit rule, drift chosen by rehearsing each flip ahead.
  Third entry 047 (Twins): two scrolls under one rule, one cell apart; the
  difference spreads as a red wedge or closes. The reader flips rule bits and
  nudges cells. (Rules one bit apart was tried first: every bit gets asked at
  once on a random row, so twins always parted instantly. Dropped.) Rest at three.

- **Soundings** (game) — find a hidden thing in a hidden field from a few
  measurements, then see the field. Started in 006. Second entry 019 (Half
  Tide): readings include a hidden tide, reduced to low water at the end.
  Third entry 054 (Set and Drift): a rotary tidal stream sets the boat off each
  mark; a two-bearing fix shows where the lead went; the buoy drifts too. Rest at three.

- **Almanac** (tool) — pasted list → wall-calendar poster, print or PNG.
  Started in 007. Second entry 021 (Lunations): a year of moons on paper
  shaded by night length at a chosen latitude. Possible third: tides or
  eclipses computed in, or the planets' risings. Or rest at two.

- **Ten ways** (study) — one fixed scene, many constraints. Started in 008.
  Second entry 030 (Noon table): a still life lit from one side, ten new
  constraints (engraving, hatching, contours, mosaic, string art, shadows,
  spiral, paint by numbers, torn paper, form strokes). Possible third: a
  viewer-chosen pairing of two constraints, or a face/figure. Or rest at two.

- **A city, visited** (map) — a city that grows when you return. Finished in
  009. Second entry 058 (Gazetteer, run 069): reads the reader's own city from
  localStorage (`nightshift-city-v2`, read-only), rebuilds it with a copy of 009's
  generator, and writes a gazetteer: etymologies, courses, visits, character lines,
  places found and not yet found, annals. Specimen town (seed 12345, visit 7) if none.
  If 009's generator ever changes, 058's copy must change with it (bump both keys).
  Possible third: a second town on the same coast, or a guidebook walk. Or rest at two.

- **Letters** (fiction) — letters left in a watch-house drawer by watchers with
  no memory of the night before. Six letters in 005. Possible sequel: the
  boat's side (the other watch that answers the lamp), or the old brown letters.
  Second entry 010: the boat's log. Third entry 049 (Under the lining): the old
  brown letters, top of the pile to the first keeper's pencil on the drawer bottom;
  the lost ledger line is "Answer the boat". Rest at three.

- **House rules** (poem) — erasure poems inside one fixed notice, a reader
  per tab, plus the viewer's own. Started in 014. Possible sequels: another
  found text (a timetable, a recipe, a will), or erasures that change with
  the hour.

- **Changes** (sound) — synthesised tower bells ringing plain hunt / plain bob,
  viewer calls bobs and singles, or a conductor rings the 720. Started in 015.
  Possible sequels: other methods or more bells (Grandsire on five/seven,
  minor methods with different lead ends), or a handbell version where the
  viewer rings one bell by tapping in time.
  Second entry 032 (Rope): five bells ring, the viewer rings the sixth by
  tapping; striking chart at the end. Possible third: handbells (two bells per
  ringer), or a ringer who listens and follows the viewer's timing. Or rest at two.

- **Drawdown** (loom) — editable four-shaft weaving draft with cloth preview.

  colour orders over a fixed weave. Possible third: eight shafts, or a draft
  read off a finished cloth. Or rest at two.

- **Hoist** (signal) — type a line, it flies as international code flags,

  third: morse by lamp, or a hoist to decode. Or rest at two.

- **Size** (craft) — a marbling bath: drops, stylus, comb, four traditional

  (Chancery): an italic hand with branching arches, automatic joins, capitals

- **Gnomon** (instrument) — horizontal sundial for any latitude, date lines,

  Second entry 053 (Shepherd's Dial): a pillar dial, side view plus its surface

  dial for any facing. Or rest at two.

- **Index** (fiction) — a story told only as the back-of-book index of a lost

  Possible sequels: another book's index (a cookbook, a ship's manual), or the

- **Glean** (board game) — 6×6 field of sheaves and thistles; you take rows,

  sequels: a hex field with three gleaners, or a field where taken squares

- **Crease** (stretchtext) — a short story folded into nine sentences that

- **Interlace** (pattern) — knotwork on a square grid; tap lines to place
  breaks, cords traced and counted, over/under from the grid. Started in 048.
  Possible sequels: a triangular or radial grid, key patterns (step/spiral),
  or a panel that must be made one cord in few breaks (a puzzle). Or leave it single.

- **Fold and cut** (papercut) — a folded slice you cut with loops and holes,
  opened beside it. Started in 040. Second entry 050 (Paper Dolls): an accordion
  strip, cut around / cut out, a flood-fill count says chain, pairs or loose.
  Possible third: a square sheet folded corner to corner, or the one-straight-cut
  puzzle. Or rest at two.

- **Planisphere** (instrument) — a star wheel for an invented sky: date ring,
  hour ring, horizon window recut for latitude 20–66°N, sixteen figures with
  one-line lore. Started in 041; kin to Gnomon (same sun-time arithmetic).
  Possible sequels: the southern window (other side of the wheel), planets that
  wander through the figures, or the sky's own almanac. Or leave it single.

- **Fern frost** (art) — frost ferns on a four-pane window: stems, 60° shoots, barbs;
  breath clears and refreezes, scratches grow seams, specks grow stars. Started in 056.
  Possible sequels: other window ice (rime, a pond's black ice cracking, hoar on a
  leaf), or growth of another kind (lichen, salt crystals in a drying dish). Or leave it single.
  Second entry 067 (Map Lichen, piece 056): six species on a carved sandstone boundary stone
  over 1868–2068, colonies spreading at their own rates and stopping where another got first;
  map lichen's black rims; the widest map lichen dates the stone. Possible third: salt crystals
  in a drying dish, or a pond's black ice. Or rest at two.

- **Both ways** (poem) — reversos: lines that make a second poem read upward, turned by
  a button, stops and capitals flashing as they move. Started in 057. Possible sequels:
  a two-voice reverso (two speakers, one each way), or a longer sequence. Kin to House
  rules (a poem with a small machine in it). Or leave it single.

- **Kite** (game) — a kite on a line, three days of invented wind; pull in, let out, or hold
  with a slipping spool; gusts visible in the grass. Started in 059; kin to Gather (games of
  feel). Possible sequels: a stunt kite with two lines, a kite fight, or a day with a
  thermal. Or leave it single.

- **Crossdating** (puzzle) — tree-ring dating: slide a core along a master chronology,
  t-value meter, bark edge / sapwood windows, a reused timber, then when was it built. Started
  in 062. Possible sequels: a chronology you build yourself by bridging overlapping samples
  back in time, or a panel painting / a ship's timbers dated the same way. Or leave it single.

- **Proving** (poem) — a sestina with its end-word turn drawn as a braid, plus a blank scaffold
  for six of the reader's own words. Started in 063; kin to Both ways and House rules (poems with
  a small machine). Possible sequels: a villanelle (two refrains), a pantoum (lines that return),
  or a canzone. Or leave it single.

- **Walling** (game) — rebuild a gap in a dry stone wall from a sorted pile: cross every joint,
  through band on course four, coping, then a hundred winters drop stacked running joints into a gap.
  Started in 068 (piece 057). Possible sequels: the end view (two faces, batter, hearting), a cheek
  end or a lunky (sheep creep), or a stone stile. Or leave it single.

- **Bias** (game) — crown green bowls: biased woods and jack, a crowned and undulating green with
  contours, mat follows the jack, Wren plans by trying a fan of deliveries. Started in 071 (piece 060);
  kin to Gather and Kite (games of feel; Kite has a heart). Possible sequels: a flat green (lawn bowls,
  rinks, a ditch you can't bowl into), a doubles game, or a green that changes with rain. Or leave it single.
